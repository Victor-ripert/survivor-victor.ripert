# Terms of Service — draft (AD-8)

**Status: draft, not published.** To be reviewed by Florine Pontaillac alongside
the processing register (EW-7) before any sending or publication, as she requested.
This document describes what the platform actually does as of September 4, 2026 —
not an intention.

## 1. Purpose

ProfilsActifs is a professional matching service between job seekers and
recruiters, operated on behalf of the Ministère du Job et Bonheur. It is not a
social network: the platform offers no news feed, no engagement mechanics (likes,
shares), and no real-time notifications in the strict sense.

## 2. Accounts and roles

Registration creates an account with one of the following two roles, chosen by the
person registering:

- **Job seeker** — creates a profile, publishes presentation videos, takes the
  certification questionnaire.
- **Recruiter** — browses the profile catalogue, bookmarks favorites, contacts
  candidates.

A third role, **administrator**, exists for moderation and platform management; it
is never created through self-registration and is provisioned separately.

## 3. Minimum age

Registration is restricted to people at least 16 years old as of the declared
birth date. A registration without a birth date, or declaring an age below this
threshold, is rejected. The specific regime applicable to 16-18 year-olds will be
detailed once confirmed by legal counsel; at this stage, only the minimum age check
is implemented.

Accounts created before this verification was introduced are neither suspended nor
retroactively deleted (see `docs/legal/accounts-without-birthdate.md`).

## 4. Video content and consent

Publishing a presentation video requires specific, timestamped and versioned
consent — never a pre-checked box. The exact text submitted for consent is in
`docs/legal/consent-text-v1.md`.

Videos are embedded via link (YouTube or Vimeo): ProfilsActifs hosts no video file.
Consent is revocable at any time; revocation immediately removes the association
between the video and the candidate's profile (see
`docs/legal/video-consent-revocation.md`).

## 5. Data collected at registration

First name, last name, email address, password (never stored in plain text), birth
date, chosen role. Full detail on processing, purpose and retention period is in
the processing register (EW-7), with which this document must stay consistent.

## 6. Moderation

Profiles and video content may be moderated by an administrator. A rejected
profile no longer appears in the recruiter catalogue; moderation decisions are
logged.

## 7. Account deletion

An account can be deactivated; the terms of full deletion and the fate of
associated data will be specified in a later version of this document, consistent
with the processing register.
